A Day A Word A Day

What is this?
Write one word per day. No editing. No deleting. Each word is permanent.

Colors:
Each word has its own color. How? That is the mystery.

Buttons:
📅 Calendar - Pick a date. If you never wrote a word on that day,
           it jumps to the next day that has a word.
📂⬇️ Import - Restores entries from a JSON backup.
           WARNING: This REPLACES everything currently in your diary.
           Any entries not in the backup file will be lost forever.
💾⬆️ Export - Downloads all entries as JSON.
           Do this regularly. Your diary lives only in your browser.
🏠 Today - Return to today.
❓ Help - Opens this help window.

History buttons (10, 20, 50, 100, 200, ∞):
Show past entries. The background becomes the average color of those entries.
A colored stripe shows the timeline.

Bottom bar:
Last 5 entries. Click to jump to that word.

Philosophy:
Once written, it stays. You think before you write.
Export your diary often. Browsers forget. Computers break.
Things get lost. This is life.

Technical:
All data stays in your browser. No cloud, no tracking, no login.
WARNING: Clearing your browser data deletes your diary.
WARNING: Importing a JSON file overwrites everything.
Export regularly. Keep backups in different places.
